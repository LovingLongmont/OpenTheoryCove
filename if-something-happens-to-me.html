About the book

Useful information, in one place

This book is designed to reduce the scavenger hunt that can happen when someone else suddenly needs to step in. Prompts help you document what matters, where records are kept, who should be contacted, and the details that are easy to overlook because you normally carry them in your head.

Inside

What it helps you organize

  • Identity and important records
  • People and dependents
  • Medical information and care documents
  • Home, property, vehicles, and valuables
  • Financial records and accounts
  • Digital life and online accounts
  • Pets and their care
  • Key contacts and professionals
  • Legal documents and final wishes
  • Personal notes and information only you may know
