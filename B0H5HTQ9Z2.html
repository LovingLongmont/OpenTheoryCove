A few ideas to put to work now

Start with the book before you start with KDP

One of the key lessons in this book is that publishing a successful book starts long before you upload files to Amazon. Take time to plan, prepare and think like a reader.

1.

Clarify your purpose and your reader

Be clear on why you are writing the book and who it is for. Knowing your reader will guide every decision, from content to cover design to marketing.

2.

Outline and organize your content

Use a clear structure that keeps readers engaged. A detailed outline now makes writing, editing and formatting much easier later.

3.

Plan your format and design

Choose the right trim size, paper type and interior layout for your content. Think about the reader experience, not just what is easiest.

4.

Research keywords and categories early

Look at similar books on Amazon to understand what is working. This helps you make stronger decisions for your title, subtitle, description and categories.

5.

Set realistic goals and a launch plan

Publishing is just the beginning. Create a simple marketing plan so your book gets noticed once it is live.

Try this: Before you open your KDP account, write down your book's purpose, target reader, three comparable titles and one key benefit your book offers. This will make every next step easier.
