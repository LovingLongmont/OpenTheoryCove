What can you use your journal for?

Daily reflection

Write down what happened, what stood out and what you want to remember.

Ideas and notes

Goals and progress

Simple journal prompts

  • What happened or what is on my mind?
  • Why does it matter to me?
  • What is a next step I want to take?
