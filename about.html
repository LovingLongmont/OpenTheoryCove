About Open Theory Cove

Open Theory Cove is an independent publishing and creative studio built around useful ideas. We create practical guides, guided workbooks, journals and creative resources designed to help people organize what matters, learn something useful, explore an idea, or turn a plan into action.

The name reflects the way the Cove approaches creative work. Open is an invitation to remain curious. Theory leaves room for questions, possibilities and ideas that can grow. Cove represents a protected place to pause long enough to turn thought into something useful.

Our catalog is intentionally broad because useful ideas do not belong to only one category. A project may begin with preparedness, budgeting, small business, journaling, creativity or a question worth exploring. What connects the collection is the goal of making each resource approachable, practical and made to be used.
