About Open Theory Cove

Independent by design

A publishing and creative brand built around practical ideas, useful tools and the belief that good ideas should become something real.

Create • Restore • Inspire

Open Theory Cove brings together books, journals, notebooks, genealogy tools and creative products under one philosophy: create what matters, restore what has been neglected, and inspire the next step.

Formerly In Spirit Publishing: Earlier books and products may still show In Spirit Publishing, Michelle Skolyak, or Independently Published in their retailer metadata. Open Theory Cove is the current brand name and the home of both the legacy catalog and new releases.

The website is organized around published products only. Drafts or titles marked unpublished in the KDP bookshelf are not included in the public catalog.
