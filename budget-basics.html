Budget Basics

A Beginner's Guide to Managing Money at Home and Growing Your Side Hustle or Small Business

Written for complete beginners, this practical, judgment-free guide walks readers through taking control of household money while building a side hustle or small business that makes financial sense.

Perfect for:

  • First-time budgeters
  • Freelancers and gig workers
  • Etsy sellers and service businesses
  • Side hustlers learning to separate business and personal money
  • Anyone who wants a clear money system without spreadsheet overwhelm

Each chapter uses plain-English explanations, worked examples and action steps so readers know what to do next.
