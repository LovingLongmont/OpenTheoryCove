A homepage for the way you work

CoveScapes gives you one place to start a work session and find the sites you use most. Put your favorite tools in quick shortcuts and group everything else by what you do.

  • Run your business. Group your own websites, admin dashboards, shop listings, selling platforms, shipping tools, and order dashboards.
  • Make and publish. Keep design tools, writing apps, image libraries, project boards, and content planners together.
  • Manage your social accounts. Save the profiles, scheduling tools, and analytics pages you use for work.
  • Keep fewer tabs open. Save the pages you return to throughout a work session, then open each one when you need it.

Change the scenery, keep your links

Each CoveScapes edition pairs your shortcuts and categories with an illustrated scene. Choose a quiet mountain creek, a moonlit woodland, or another design that feels right for your workspace. When you want a change, export a backup and restore it in a compatible CoveScapes edition to bring your links and settings with you.

An easy gift to send from anywhere

Give family or friends a desktop homepage that suits their interests. Available editions are instant digital downloads, so you can send one to someone out of state with no shipping fees. It’s also a gift you can send at the last minute.
