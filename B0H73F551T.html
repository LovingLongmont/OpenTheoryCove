A few ideas to put to work now

Start with a 15-minute Etsy visibility check

One of the core lessons in Etsy Unlocked is that getting found and getting bought are two different problems. Before changing your product, first figure out which problem you actually have.

1. Think like the shopper, not the maker

Choose one product and write down three to five phrases a customer might search without using your exact product name. Think about the recipient, occasion, room, style or problem the product solves. A seller may think “macrame wall hanging,” while a shopper may search “boho nursery decor.”

2. Look for blind spots in search

Open Etsy in a private or incognito window and search those phrases. Look at the first few rows. If comparable products appear but yours does not, you may have a query-matching gap rather than a product-quality problem.

Try this: Pick one listing that you believe deserves more sales. Write down its current views, visits, orders and conversion rate for the last 30 days before changing anything. That gives you a real baseline instead of relying on memory.

3. Check whether your listing speaks the buyer’s language

Review the title, all 13 tag slots, category and attributes. They should help Etsy understand what the item is and the different ways a shopper might search for it. The goal is not repetition or keyword stuffing; it is clear relevance.

4. Separate low visibility from low conversion

If very few people are seeing the listing, focus on discoverability: title, tags, category, attributes and traffic. If shoppers are seeing and clicking but not buying, look instead at photography, price, shipping, description, reviews and trust. Fixing the wrong part of the funnel can waste weeks.

5. Give changes enough time to teach you something

The book repeatedly uses 30- and 90-day tracking because daily reactions make it too easy to change several things at once. Record what you changed and when, then compare the same metrics later. Improvement becomes easier when you can tell which change actually helped.
