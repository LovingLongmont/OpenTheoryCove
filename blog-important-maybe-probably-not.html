Journaling Practice • Mental Clutter

Important • Maybe • Probably Not

Not every thought deserves the same amount of attention. A useful journal can be a place to save what matters, park what might matter later, and unload what definitely does not need to keep circling in your head.

One reason people stop journaling is that every entry starts to feel like it should be meaningful. But most days are full of mixed material: a real task, a strange idea, something funny someone said, an unresolved worry, a grocery reminder and a thought that will seem completely irrelevant tomorrow. That is normal.

The goal of this practice is not to make every thought important. It is to give each thought somewhere to go.

The three-part practice

1. Important

Write the thought down. If it requires action, a decision or attention, mark it with a star. Then add one next step. Keep the step small enough that you could actually do it.

2. Maybe

Some thoughts are interesting but not ready. Keep them without forcing a conclusion. Add one sentence about why the idea caught your attention, then leave it alone for now.

3. Probably Not

Some thoughts are simply mental clutter. Write them down so your brain does not have to keep holding them. Cross them out if that feels satisfying, or leave them on the page and move on.

Why this can help

Writing something down creates a little distance between you and the thought. Instead of asking, “Why am I thinking about this?” you can ask a simpler question: “What category does this belong in right now?” That small shift can make a crowded mind feel easier to manage.

It also gives random ideas a fair chance. Something that looks unimportant today may become useful later. At the same time, recording a thought does not obligate you to act on it.

Try these prompts

  • What do I keep trying to remember without writing it down?
  • What idea seems silly but still interests me?
  • What can I safely stop thinking about today?
  • What needs action, and what only needs a place to be stored?
  • What small thing has been taking up a ridiculous amount of mental space?
  • What would I like tomorrow-me to find on this page?
