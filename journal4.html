Why This Journal Exists

Not every useful thought arrives looking important. Sometimes the odd idea, passing observation or messy note becomes the thing you are glad you saved. This journal makes room for those in-between thoughts without demanding that you decide what they mean first.

Important • Maybe • Probably Not

A three-step sorting practice for a crowded mind

1
★

Important

Write down the thought exactly as it arrived. If it needs action, a decision, a reminder or more attention, mark it with a star and add one clear next step.

2
?

Maybe

If you are not sure what the thought means yet, keep it without solving it. Add one sentence about why it caught your attention and give yourself permission to revisit it later.

3
☁

Probably Not

If it is just mental clutter, write it anyway — then let the page hold it for you. Cross it out, laugh at it, or leave it there. Not every thought needs another job.
