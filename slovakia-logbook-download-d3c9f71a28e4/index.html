How to print

  1. Choose US Letter (8.5 × 11 inches), portrait, and Actual Size or 100%. Use Fit if your print preview shows clipped edges.
  2. Pages 1–2 are the optional cover and printing guide. Pages 6–8 are the research index, and pages 9–76 are the numbered record spreads. Page 77 is for continuation notes.
  3. Print matching detail and notes sheets for each record. Print single-sided if you plan to add the sheets to a binder.
