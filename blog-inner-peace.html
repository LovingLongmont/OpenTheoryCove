Inner Peace: A Bilingual Reflection

Sometimes journaling begins not with a question, but with a few quiet words that help us return to the present moment.

Вътрешен мир Тишината живее в мен, като утринна светлина. Не търси път през шумния свят — тя чака тихо у дома. Оставям вчера да си тръгне, не моля утре да дойде сега. Поемам дъх и в този миг намирам своята душа. Като планина под ясно небе, като море след бурен ден, мирът не идва отдалече — той винаги е бил във мен.
Inner Peace Silence lives within me, like the light of morning. It does not seek a path through the noisy world— it waits quietly at home. I let yesterday depart, and do not ask tomorrow to arrive today. I breathe, and in this moment I find my soul again. Like a mountain beneath a clear sky, like the sea after a storm, peace does not come from somewhere far away— it has always lived within me.

Turn the reflection into a journal practice

After reading the poem, write for five minutes about one thing you are ready to release from yesterday and one thing you want to be fully present for today. Keep the focus small and immediate rather than trying to solve everything at once.

You can also finish the sentence, “Peace feels possible when…” and write whatever comes to mind without editing it.
