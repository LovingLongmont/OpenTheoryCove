Why This Journal Exists

Not every thought needs to be polished, solved or explained. Sometimes clarity begins by getting what is in your head onto a page. This journal was made to give messy, funny, difficult, hopeful and completely random thoughts a place to land.

Draw It • Write It • Let It Go

A three-step reset for a busy mind

1
✎

Draw It

Without worrying about artistic skill, sketch what your mind feels like right now. Use shapes, symbols, lines, faces, objects or complete nonsense. The point is expression, not perfection.

2
☁

Write It

Look at what you drew and write whatever comes to mind. What does the image represent? What thought keeps returning? What feels loud, unfinished, exciting or heavy?

3
↗

Let It Go

Circle one thing you want to keep, underline one thing you want to change, and cross out one thing you are ready to stop carrying today.
