What can you use your journal for?

Revisit the experience

Start with what happened and the details you remember.

Explore what it meant

Make room for the emotions, questions and turning points that come up.

Consider what comes next

Write about what you want to keep, change or understand better.

Simple journal prompts

  • Which detail do I remember most clearly?
  • How did this experience change the way I see things?
  • What do I want to tell my future self about this moment?

Work through the story at your pace

Begin with one prompt and give yourself time to think. Revisit earlier entries whenever a new detail or perspective comes to mind.
