Our Publishing History

From In Spirit Publishing to Open Theory Cove

One creative catalog, a new name, and the same independent spirit.

The name changed. The publishing history did not.

Open Theory Cove is the current name of the independent publishing and creative brand previously known as In Spirit Publishing.

Many earlier books, journals, notebooks, genealogy research logs and creative products were originally released under the In Spirit Publishing name or under Michelle Skolyak’s author name. Those published works remain part of the same catalog and are being brought together here under Open Theory Cove.

What you may see on older retailer listings: In Spirit Publishing, Michelle Skolyak, or Independently Published. Open Theory Cove now serves as the home for both the legacy catalog and new releases.

The catalog is organized by product type so that similar journals, notebooks and cover variations can be browsed together rather than hidden in one long list.
