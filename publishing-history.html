Our Publishing History

One creative catalog, a new name, and the same independent spirit.

From In Spirit Publishing to Open Theory Cove

Open Theory Cove is the current name of the independent publishing and creative brand previously known as In Spirit Publishing.

Many earlier books, journals, notebooks, genealogy research logs and creative products were originally released under the In Spirit Publishing name or under Michelle Skolyak’s author name. Those published works remain part of the same creative catalog and are being brought together here under Open Theory Cove.

What changed?
The brand name changed. The published history did not. Older listings may still show In Spirit Publishing, Michelle Skolyak, or independently published as the publisher or creator. Open Theory Cove now serves as the home for both the legacy catalog and new releases.

The goal of this website is to make that catalog easier to browse by type—books, journals, notebooks, genealogy tools, logbooks and other creative works—while preserving the history of the titles that came before the name change.
