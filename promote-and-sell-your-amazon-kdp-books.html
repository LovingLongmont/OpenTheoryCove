What the guide covers

Improve your Amazon listing

Review your title, cover, description, keywords, and categories so readers can find the book and understand what it offers.

Reach readers on a small budget

Build an advance reader team and plan free promotion through email, social media, Goodreads, and other channels.

Build a repeatable promotion plan

Decide when paid ads make sense, use AI and automation to reduce repetitive work, and track what works across launches.

Frequently asked questions

Who is this guide for?

Self-published authors planning a launch or looking for ways to reach new readers after publication.

Does the guide cover free and paid promotion?

Yes. It starts with free strategies and explains when to consider Amazon Advertising, BookBub promotions, and social media ads.
