What can you use your journal for?

Daily reflection

Write down what happened, what stood out and what you want to remember.

Ideas and notes

Give passing thoughts, plans and lists a place you can return to.

Goals and progress

Record a next step and check back to see what changed.

Simple journal prompts

  • What is on my mind today?
  • What do I want to remember?
  • What is one thing I can do next?

Start with one thought

Date the page and write a few lines about what is on your mind. Return when there is something else you want to record.
