Why This Journal Exists

Creative thoughts are easy to lose when we wait for them to become perfect. This journal is a reminder that unfinished ideas count. A sentence, image, question or fragment can be enough to begin — and sometimes the most useful thing you can do is simply spill the ink.

Spill • Circle • Shape

A three-step practice for turning loose thoughts into something worth keeping

1
✒

Spill

Write for five minutes without editing. Put down the fragments, unfinished sentences, observations and ideas exactly as they arrive. Your only job is to keep the pen moving.

2
◯

Circle

Read what you wrote once. Circle one word, sentence or image that still has energy. Do not choose the most polished part — choose the part that makes you want to keep looking.

3
✦

Shape

Use what you circled as the beginning of something new: a paragraph, poem, sketch idea, question, scene or journal entry. Give the thought ten more minutes before deciding what it should become.
