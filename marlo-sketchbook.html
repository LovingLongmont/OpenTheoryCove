What can you use this sketchbook for?

Drawing practice

Practice lines, shapes, and shading.

Quick sketches

Doodling

Fill a page with patterns, little characters, or whatever comes to mind.

Simple drawing prompts

  • Choose an object nearby and draw its outline.
  • Sketch the same object from a second angle.
  • Draw a small scene using only lines and dots.
