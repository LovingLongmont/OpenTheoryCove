A journaling technique to try

The Notice • Name • Next Method

A simple three-step practice for understanding what is happening inside you, finding clarity and turning reflection into one small meaningful action.

1

Notice

Notice what is on your mind right now. Write without trying to fix or edit anything yet.

2

Name

Give the thought or feeling a name so it becomes easier to understand.

3

Next

Ask yourself: What is one small, kind next step I can take?

Try this: A 5-Minute Journal Practice
Set a timer for five minutes. Write about what is on your mind using Notice • Name • Next.

What five honest minutes can look like

“I noticed I have been feeling anxious about everything on my to-do list. My next step is to choose just one task for today and focus on completing it.”
