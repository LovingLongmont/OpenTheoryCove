The Notice • Name • Next Method

A simple three-step journaling practice for understanding what is happening inside you, finding clarity and turning reflection into one small meaningful action.

1. Notice

Notice what is on your mind right now. Write without trying to fix or edit anything yet.

2. Name

Give the thought or feeling a name so it becomes easier to understand.

3. Next

Ask yourself: What is one small, kind next step I can take?

6 journal prompts to get started

  1. What made me smile today?
  2. What am I grateful for right now?
  3. What am I looking forward to?
  4. What has been on my mind?
  5. What small step can I take toward a goal?
  6. What do I want to remember?
